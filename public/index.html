Services

Four services that build on each other

Most clients start with the audit and move on to the next step when they're ready. We scope and quote every project in writing before work begins.

  1. Start here

    AI Readiness & Data Privacy Audit

    A two-week audit that makes AI safe, simple and worth it for your business. We set clear rules for protecting your data, review the AI tools your team already uses, and find the admin work that's ready to be automated.

    • A one-page Staff AI Use Policy your team will actually read
    • A Data Privacy Risk Register, based on BC's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and the federal PIPEDA
    • Three workflow blueprints, with the hours each could save per month
    • A 60-minute leadership debrief and a full handover of every document
    $1,500 CADfixed fee, two weeks

  2. Workflow Automation

    Simple, dependable automations built inside the tools you already use, with Make or Zapier. Website enquiries land in your CRM, payments match to invoices, and meeting notes get filed without anyone copying and pasting. Where your usual software views fall short, we can add a simple dashboard or client portal.

    • Built in your own accounts with no custom code, so everything we create belongs to you
    • Personal details removed before data goes to an AI service, and AI tools set to keep no data
    • Short video guides for your team and a stability warranty after launch
    $1,500 – $11,500 CADdepending on the size of the setup

  3. Privacy Management Program

    Practical privacy governance sized for your organization. We map where personal information lives, write your privacy policy and staff rules, review your key software, and set up clear steps for breaches and access requests.

    • Public privacy policy, staff privacy standard, and consent and intake forms
    • Privacy Impact Assessments: structured reviews of how a tool handles personal information
    • Breach response and access-request procedures you can follow under pressure
    $2,000 – $14,000 CADdepending on team size and how sensitive your information is. 3 to 6 weeks.

  4. Staff AI Readiness & Safety Training

    Live workshops, online or on site in British Columbia, that help your team use AI with confidence and common sense. People learn what stays out of AI tools, how to write a good prompt, and how to check the answers.

    • Prompt playbooks and one-page desk guides for each department
    • Short video tutorials your team can come back to
    • A walk-through of your own AI Use Policy, so it becomes a habit
    $1,250 – $7,000 CADdepending on team size

Prices are a guide. We confirm the scope and price in writing before we start, and each service has a clear list of what's not included. Your own software subscriptions, such as Make, Zapier or AI usage fees, are separate. We bring certified privacy expertise, not legal counsel.

How we work

The Alignment Method™

Every project follows the same five steps, so you always know what's happening and why.

  1. Understand

    We listen to your team, watch the real work, and map where the day gets stuck.

    You walk away with: an Operational Flow Map

  2. Connect

    We build simple, behind-the-scenes links between the apps you already use.

    You walk away with: live automations

  3. Simplify

    We make tools and information visual, clear and easy to reach.

    You walk away with: a dashboard that answers "where are we at?"

  4. Protect

    We put plain-English safe-use rules and privacy procedures in writing.

    You walk away with: a one-page Staff AI Use Policy and a Privacy Risk Register

  5. Equip

    We train your team with short video guides so they can run the system with confidence.

    You walk away with: a System Playbook and a video library

About

Hi, I'm Ninian.

I'm Ninian Dougall, B.A. (Hons), CIPP/C. That's the Certified Information Privacy Professional credential for Canada.

I started on the privacy side of healthcare, working at Victoria Hospice. There I built the privacy management program, completed six privacy impact assessments, and set up a process for answering freedom-of-information requests.

I also test AI systems for mistakes, made-up answers and data leaks. Both kinds of work lead to the same place. The most useful technology keeps getting built for companies with enterprise budgets, and smaller businesses are left with slow processes and real privacy risk. N-Deavour is my way of changing that.
