Why now

Your team is probably already using AI. Most businesses don't have a plan for it yet.

  • 24% of Canadian workers who use generative AI say they've entered proprietary company data into public AI tools.
  • 37% of those workers say they aren't aware of any controls from their employer.
  • 33% of Canadian businesses say they regularly give staff privacy training.

None of this is a reason to panic. It's a reason to put simple rules in writing. A one-page AI use policy and a clear list of what stays out of AI tools covers most of it, and that's where the audit starts.

Sources: KPMG Canada, Generative AI Adoption Index, 2024 (2,183 workers; the first two figures are shares of generative AI users). Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, Survey of Canadian businesses on privacy-related issues, 2023–24 (800 businesses).
