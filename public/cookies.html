Cookie Notice

Last updated October 1, 2026

This notice explains how ndeavour.ca uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. Our own cookies

This site doesn't set cookies of its own. It doesn't use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking, and it doesn't store information in your browser's local storage. Sending the contact form doesn't require cookies.

2. Third-party requests

The site loads everything it needs, including its typeface, from ndeavour.ca itself. It makes no requests to other companies' servers while you browse. Our hosting provider, Netlify, keeps standard server logs of visits (see the Privacy Policy).

3. Your choices

You can block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings. Because this site sets no cookies of its own, no cookie consent banner is shown.

4. Changes

If we add analytics or other technologies that use cookies, we'll update this notice and ask for your consent where required. Questions: nindougall@gmail.com.
