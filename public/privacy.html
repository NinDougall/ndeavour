Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

This policy explains what personal information N-Deavour Alignment collects through this website (ndeavour.ca), why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. N-Deavour Alignment is a sole proprietorship operated by Ninian Dougall in British Columbia, Canada. We handle personal information in line with British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

1. Who is responsible

Ninian Dougall is responsible for personal information collected through this site. Questions, access requests, corrections and deletion requests go to nindougall@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • What you send us. If you use the contact form: your name, email address, an optional area of interest, and the description you write. If you email us directly: your email address and whatever you include.
  • Technical information from your visit. Our hosting provider automatically records your IP address, browser and device type, the pages requested, and the date and time. We use this to deliver the site and keep it secure.

We don't ask for payment details, government identification or health information through this site. Please don't include client, patient or other sensitive personal information in the contact form.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and, if you wish, talk about possible work together.
  • To keep a record of our correspondence and remember to follow up, in our client records system (see sections 4 and 8).
  • To keep the website working and secure.

We don't sell or rent personal information, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

4. Who we share it with

We use these service providers to run this site, our correspondence and our client records:

  • Netlify, Inc. hosts the website. It also receives and stores contact form submissions so that we can read them and be notified by email. Netlify also hosts the screens of our private client records system, but none of the records themselves.
  • Make (make.com) passes each contact form submission from Netlify into our client records system. It handles your enquiry in the European Union, is set not to keep a copy afterwards, and doesn't receive your IP address or browser details.
  • Supabase, Inc. stores our client records, including your enquiry and our notes about our conversations, in its data centre in Canada (Montréal).
  • Google provides the email service (Gmail) that receives form notifications and our correspondence.

This site's typeface is served from the site itself, and the site loads no scripts, fonts or other resources from third parties. Netlify and Google may store or process information in the United States, and Make in the European Union, where it may be accessible to courts and authorities under local law. Our client records are stored in Canada. We may also disclose personal information where the law requires it.

5. Cookies and tracking

We don't use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking on this site. See the Cookie Notice for details.

6. Other N-Deavour tools

If we offer a self-assessment or other tool on a separate website, that tool has its own privacy notice. This policy covers only ndeavour.ca.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires:

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work are deleted from our client records once 12 months have passed without contact.
  • If you become a client, we keep your records for 3 years after our last work together. Invoices and payment records are kept for 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to, as the Canada Revenue Agency requires.
  • Copies of your enquiry in our website host's form inbox and in our email are deleted on the same schedule.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner at any time, except records the law requires us to keep.

8. How we protect it

The site is served over HTTPS. Our client records system is stored in Canada and encrypted, and signing in to it takes a strong password plus a code from an authenticator app. It signs out automatically after 15 minutes without activity. Only Ninian Dougall has access, and each time a record is opened, that is logged. No online system is completely secure, so please keep sensitive details out of the contact form.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it and stop using it (withdraw your consent). Email nindougall@gmail.com. We'll respond within 30 days. If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia (oipc.bc.ca).

10. Client engagements

Personal information we handle while carrying out paid work for a client is governed by the written agreement for that engagement, in addition to the law. Our clients' contact and billing details are kept in the same Canadian client records system described above.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
