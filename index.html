Example solution

Financial Planner AI is one kind of custom solution.

A financial planner AI can help a business owner organize budgets, forecast cash flow, compare scenarios, and prepare decision-ready summaries. It is an example of the custom AI workflows N-Deavour can design around your real operations.

  • Turn invoices, goals, and recurring costs into plain-language summaries.
  • Generate cash-flow scenarios without switching between disconnected spreadsheets.
  • Flag missing information before a human makes the final decision.
  • Use privacy-aware prompts and workflows for sensitive business data.
