Foundational Philosophy

Advanced, secure AI shouldn’t only be available to giant corporations.

N-Deavour was founded by Ninian Dougall—a play on the initials N.D., and a commitment to constantly endeavouring to improve. The practice grew from a simple frustration: the most useful technology keeps getting locked behind enterprise budgets, leaving smaller businesses to manage with outdated processes and real privacy risk.

The name is personal by design. The work is, too.

  1. 01

    Healthcare safety & records privacy

    Started at a local hospice clearing out manual paperwork so frontline staff could focus on patients, while keeping sensitive medical records completely private. The first lesson: removing administrative friction is an act of care.

  2. 02

    AI red-teaming & data privacy testing

    Moved into testing advanced AI systems—finding critical data leaks, hallucination errors, and privacy vulnerabilities before they reached real users. The second lesson: AI is only safe when someone is actively looking for what can go wrong.

  3. 03

    Bringing it to small businesses

    Both threads converged into one practice. AI is simply a practical tool for handling repetitive tasks and safeguarding business data. Small businesses deserve the same access to it that enterprise teams have always had.

Human-centric layer

Operational change is hard on people. That deserves a dedicated service.

Restructuring workflows, adopting new technology, or navigating a major operational shift creates human friction. Burnout, decision fatigue, and unprocessed stress do not fix themselves when you deploy a new tool.

Emotional Support Consulting and Debriefing is a structured, confidential service for individuals and teams working through high-pressure transitions. It belongs in the operational toolkit alongside process improvement.

Post-transition debriefing

Structured sessions following significant operational changes, technology adoptions, or difficult workplace events to restore clarity and reduce residual friction.

Burnout prevention & resilience support

Ongoing support for owners and team members navigating sustained high-pressure environments—identifying early signals and creating practical routines before burnout compounds.

Freelance emotional support debriefing

Confidential one-to-one debriefing for individuals working in demanding or emotionally intensive roles who need a structured space to process and reset.
